# Management Summary: Student Handbook Rules & Fines

This document provides a distilled version of the residential rules and penalty rates as per the UNITEN Student Handbook.

## 1. Cleaning Standards (RM10 per item)
Wardens should check these items during routine or surprise inspections. Failure to clean any of the following results in a flat RM10 fine per instance:
- Floor, Wall, Door
- Cupboard, Desk, Bed
- Mirror, Window
- Kitchen Cabinets, Sink
- Rubbish bin

## 2. Infrastructure & Safety (Immediate Eviction Risk)
Certain violations compromise the safety of the residence and carry high penalty weight or immediate disciplinary action:
- **Gas/Portable Stoves**: **RM300 Fine** + Potential Immediate Eviction.
- **Fire Equipment Misuse**: **RM50 Fine** + Potential Immediate Eviction.
- **Prohibited Safety Items**: **RM50 - RM300** based on severity.
- **Smoking**: **RM250 Fine**.

## 3. High-Value Damage Rates
Damages to high-value assets should be documented with photos immediately:
- **Air Conditioner**: RM1,500
- **Digital Door Lock**: RM1,500
- **Fridge**: RM1,000
- **Premium Room Furniture**: RM1,000 per item (Bed, Cupboard, Study Table).
- **Water Heater**: RM700

## 4. Conduct & Security
- **Squatters**: RM50 Fine (But should trigger security review/eviction).
- **Indecent Clothing**: RM50 Fine.
- **Duplicating Keys**: RM50 Fine.
- **Loss of Card/Keys/Tag**: RM30.

---
**Note**: The Management reserves the right to take necessary action it deems fit beyond these fines for repeat offenders or serious safety breaches.
